## Investigation of the failure traces -> one specific hypothesis

late improvement rate); if improving coordinated mutations are predictable,
pre-screening K = 8 of SAMFEO's own structured mutations before the
partition-function fold should buy quality per fold. Non-neural control first:
energy filter (lowest E(target) among the K children; 0.08 ms per child).
